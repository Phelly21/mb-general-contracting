  • Lot & site clearing — open up wooded lots for a home, shop, barn or driveway

  • Brush & undergrowth removal — clear tangled understory and invasive growth
  • Fence line clearing — reclaim and open up property and pasture lines

  • Tree & stump removal — take out problem trees and grind or dig stumps
  • Trail & access clearing — cut in trails and access lanes across your property

Forestry Mulching = Less Mess

Instead of piling and burning, mulching grinds vegetation right where it stands and leaves a clean layer of mulch that protects the soil and controls regrowth. Less disturbance, no giant burn piles, and a finished look faster.

Clearing Straight Into Site Work

Once it's cleared, we can go right into excavating, driveway install and drainage — one crew taking your raw lot all the way to build-ready.

Land Clearing FAQs

What does land clearing cost per acre?

It depends on how thick the growth is, tree size, terrain and access. That's why we come out and look — you'll get a clear per-project or per-acre estimate with no surprises.

Do you have to haul everything off?

Not with forestry mulching — we grind most brush and small trees into mulch on site. Larger debris or full tree removal can be hauled off if you prefer.

Can you clear just enough for a house and driveway?

Absolutely. We can clear a building envelope and driveway path and leave the rest of your treeline intact.
