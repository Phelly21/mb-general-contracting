Concrete FAQs

Do you actually pour the concrete yourselves?

Yes. Concrete is done in-house by our crew — we don't sub it out. You get one company accountable for the whole job.

Can you tear out my old driveway and replace it?

We do — we'll demo and haul off the old concrete, re-prep the base, and pour a new driveway or patio in its place.

Will my new concrete crack?

All concrete moves, which is why we prep a solid base and place control joints correctly. Done right, it stays flat and holds up for the long haul.
