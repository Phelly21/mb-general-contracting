Getting water and utilities from the source to the structure is dirty, precise work — and it has to be done right the first time, at the correct depth, with proper bedding and backfill. MB General Contracting installs underground water lines and utility runs for rural homes, new builds and shops across Phelps County.

Underground Utility Services

  • Water service lines — from the meter or main to the house
  • Well-to-house waterlines — run new supply lines from a well to the home
  • Waterline repair & replacement — replace old, leaking or undersized lines
  • Utility trenching — trenches for electric, conduit and other underground services
  • Sewer & septic lines — line runs and connections
  • New-construction utilities — get a new build ready for water and power
  • Backfill & restoration — trenches backfilled, compacted and cleaned up

Depth and Backfill Matter

Lines set too shallow freeze; trenches backfilled wrong settle and sag. We install to the correct depth with proper bedding and compaction so your lines are protected and your ground stays put.

Can't Open a Trench? Bore It.

When a line needs to cross a finished driveway, road or landscaping, we can run it with directional drilling instead of tearing up the surface.

For Homeowners & Builders

Whether you're replacing a failing waterline to an existing home or setting up utilities for a new build near St. James, Rolla or St. Robert, we get the underground work done cleanly and correctly.

Waterline & Utility FAQs

Can you run a new waterline from my well to my house?

Yes — that's a common job for us. We trench (or bore) the line at proper depth and get your water where it needs to go.

My waterline keeps leaking. Replace or repair?

We'll assess it — sometimes a repair is enough, but an old or undersized line is usually better replaced. We'll give you the honest call.

Do you handle utilities for a new build?

We do — trenching and installing water and utility runs so your new home or shop is ready to hook up.
