Directional Drilling / HDD Boring

Directional Drilling in St. James & Rolla, MO

Need to get a line to the other side of a driveway, road or finished yard without tearing it up? Directional drilling bores underneath — no trench across your surface.

📞 Free EstimateSend Project Details

Directional drilling — also called horizontal directional drilling (HDD) or boring — is a trenchless method for installing underground lines. Instead of digging an open trench across a surface, we bore a path beneath it and pull the line through. It's the answer when a waterline, electric run or conduit needs to cross something you don't want to dig up.

When Directional Drilling Makes Sense

  • Under driveways & parking — run a line across without cutting the concrete or gravel
  • Under roads — cross a road or lane without tearing it open
  • Under finished landscaping — protect established lawns, gardens and hardscape
  • Under sidewalks & patios
  • Waterlines, electric & conduit — bore the path and pull the line through
  • Where open trenching isn't practical — tight spots and sensitive surfaces

Less Damage, Less Restoration

Because there's no long open trench across the surface, boring means far less disturbance — and far less to repair afterward. Your driveway, road or yard stays intact.

Paired With Our Utility Work

Directional drilling works hand-in-hand with our waterline and utility installation — we bore the crossing, then tie it into the rest of the line.

Directional Drilling FAQs

What can you run with directional drilling?

Waterlines, electrical conduit and similar utility lines — anywhere you need to cross under a surface you'd rather not dig up.

Is boring more expensive than trenching?

Per foot it can cost more than open trenching, but when you factor in NOT having to tear out and replace a driveway, road or landscaping, it often comes out ahead. We'll help you weigh it.

Do you locate utilities before boring?

Yes — underground lines are located and marked before we bore, so the crossing is done safely.
