MB General Contracting has spent 15+ years running excavation equipment across south-central Missouri. Whether you're a homeowner, a builder, or a landowner with acreage, we handle the digging, earthmoving and site work that gets your project off the ground — dozers, excavators and skid steers ready for jobs big and small.

Excavation Services We Provide

  • Site preparation — clearing, stripping and grading building pads for homes, shops, barns and additions
  • Basement & footing excavation — dug to plan and to depth for new construction
  • Ponds & lakes — new pond digs, cleanouts, dams and spillways
  • Demolition & removal — old structures, foundations, concrete and debris hauled off
  • Earthmoving & hauling — dirt, rock and fill brought in or hauled out
  • Rough & finish grading — shaping lots and yards to drain and build
  • Trenching — for footings, utilities, drainage and waterlines

Built for Missouri Ground

Phelps County ground can go from topsoil to chert and rock in a hurry. We've dug it for years and bring the right machine and approach so your site is prepped solid — no surprises when the concrete or building goes in.

One Crew, Start to Finish

Because we also do drainage, driveways and concrete, your excavation flows straight into the rest of the job — no waiting on outside subs to line up.

Who We Work For

Homeowners adding a shop or garage, custom builders needing a pad and footings, farmers and rural landowners shaping ground and ponds, and anyone who needs dirt moved right the first time in St. James, Rolla, Newburg and the surrounding towns.

Excavating FAQs

Do you handle small residential jobs or just big projects?

Both. We size the equipment to the job — a mini excavator and skid steer for tight backyard work, or dozers and full-size excavators for site pads and ponds.

Can you dig a pond on my property?

Yes. We build new ponds and clean out or expand existing ones, including the dam and spillway. We'll walk the site with you to find the best location and size.

Do you provide free estimates?

Always. We come out, look at the site in person, and give you a clear estimate with no pressure. Call or text (573) 728-2550.
