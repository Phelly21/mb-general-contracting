Local Ground We Know

Working In and Around St. James

St. James sits in Phelps County right on the historic Route 66 alignment, with Highway 68 running south out of town toward Salem and I-44 just a minute away. It's a community of roughly 4,000 people known for Maramec Spring Park, its grape and wine heritage, and one of Missouri's largest wineries — but drive a few minutes in any direction and you're in the country, on gravel drives, small farms and wooded acreage near the headwaters of the Meramec River.

That mix is exactly what shapes the work here. In-town projects deal with older lots, mature trees and existing utilities that have to be located and worked around. Out on acreage, we're building long driveways, clearing brush and cedar, running waterlines from the road, and grading building pads on Ozark-plateau ground where you can hit chert and rock without much warning. St. James is served by a small-town public water supply district, so a lot of rural properties run their own long service lines — something we dig and bore for regularly. Whatever your corner of St. James looks like, we've almost certainly worked ground like it nearby.

What We Do Here

Our Services in St. James

One local crew, eight core services, all in-house. Here's how each one plays out on St. James property.

  • Excavating — Building pads, basements, footings and general dirt work on St. James lots and acreage. We own our dozers, excavators and skid steers, so we show up ready for both tight in-town jobs and big country sites.
  • Land Clearing — Clearing brush, cedar and overgrowth to open up pasture, food plots, homesites and fence lines on the wooded acreage around St. James. Our forestry mulcher turns a tangled tree line into usable ground in a single pass.
  • Driveway Grading — Long rural driveways off Highway 68 and the county roads take a beating from rain and washouts. We build, re-rock, crown and grade drives so they shed water and hold up year-round.
  • Concrete & Patios — Driveways, garage and shop slabs, sidewalks, patios and footings poured in-house — we don't sub it out. That means one crew owns the whole St. James job from excavation to finish.
  • Drainage — French drains, culverts, swales and yard grading to move water off homes and out of low spots. Ground near the Meramec headwaters and the local creeks can hold water, and we know how to route it away.
  • Retaining Walls — Retaining and boulder walls to hold back St. James's rolling, sometimes rocky slopes, level out a yard, or create a stable pad on ground that wants to move.
  • Waterline & Utility — Trenching and running water service, electric and other utility lines from the road to the house — a common job on the long rural runs that St. James's public water district properties often need.
  • Directional Drilling — Boring lines under driveways, roads, landscaping and the winery lanes without tearing them up, so you keep your surfaces intact.
