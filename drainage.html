Drainage Solutions

Drainage Solutions in St. James & Rolla, MO

Standing water in the yard, a wet basement, or a slope that keeps washing out? We diagnose where the water's going and build drainage that sends it where it belongs.

📞 Free EstimateSend Project Details

Between clay-heavy soils and the rolling Ozark terrain, water is a constant battle around here. MB General Contracting solves drainage problems at the source — reshaping ground and installing the right combination of drains, culverts and swales so water moves off your property instead of pooling against your home, driveway or foundation.

Drainage Services

  • French drains — subsurface drains that pull water away from low spots and foundations
  • Yard & surface drainage — regrading and swales to eliminate standing water
  • Downspout & gutter lines — carry roof water well away from the house
  • Culverts & ditching — keep driveway and roadside water flowing
  • Foundation & basement drainage — grading and drains to keep water out
  • Erosion control — stop washouts on slopes, drives and disturbed ground
  • Detention & runoff grading — manage where site water collects and releases

We Fix the Cause, Not the Symptom

A bag of mulch over a wet spot won't fix bad grading. We look at how water actually moves across your site and correct the grade and flow — so the problem is gone for good.

Pairs With Every Project

Drainage ties into driveways, retaining walls and concrete. Doing it together means one crew builds it all to drain correctly from the start.

Protect Your Property

Water is the number one enemy of driveways, foundations and yards across Phelps County. Get ahead of it with drainage built by a crew that understands local ground.

Drainage FAQs

Water pools in my yard after every rain — what fixes that?

Usually a combination of regrading the low areas and adding a French drain or swale to carry water out. We'll pinpoint the cause on site and recommend the simplest fix that actually works.

Can you keep water away from my foundation or basement?

Yes — that starts with proper grading away from the house, plus drains and downspout extensions to move roof and surface water well clear of the foundation.

Can you stop erosion on my slope or driveway?

We control erosion with regrading, drainage, rock and — where needed — a retaining wall to hold the ground in place.
