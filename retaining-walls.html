Drainage Behind the Wall

The number one reason retaining walls fail is water pressure building up behind them. We build in proper base, backfill and drainage so water can't push your wall out — it's the difference-maker.

Retaining Wall FAQs

Block or boulder — which is better?

Both work well; it comes down to look, height and budget. Boulder walls suit rural, natural settings; segmental block gives a cleaner, uniform look. We'll help you choose during the estimate.

Why do some retaining walls lean or fail?

Almost always poor base or no drainage behind the wall. We build on a compacted base with proper backfill and drainage so water pressure can't push the wall out.

Can a wall give me more flat yard?

Yes — that's one of the best uses of a retaining wall. We can cut into a slope and hold it back to create level, usable space.
