Salem & Dent County Ground

Dent County is classic Ozark country: rolling, heavily wooded hills, thin rocky soil over limestone and chert, and plenty of grade to deal with. Get toward Montauk and the Current River headwaters and you add creek bottoms, springs and land that holds water in the low spots. Out here properties are big and the drives are long — it's normal to have a quarter-mile lane back to the house through the timber. That combination of rock, slope, trees and distance is what makes rural site work its own trade. A driveway has to shed water and hold up to loaded trucks and stock trailers. A building pad has to be cut and compacted right the first time. Clearing has to be done without tearing up the ground you're trying to use. We plan the work around the way water, rock and access actually behave on Ozark land.

Salem Area FAQs

Do you travel to Salem and rural Dent County?

Yes. Salem is about a 40-mile run south from our St. James base down Highway 68, and we work throughout Dent County — in town, out toward Montauk and the Current River, and on the back tracts and hunting land in between. Give us a call and we'll set up a free on-site visit.

Can you clear wooded acreage or a hunting property?

That's some of our most common work around Salem. We clear overgrown timber and brush, open up food plots and shooting lanes, reclaim grown-over pasture, and clean up fence lines and trails. Where it fits, our forestry mulcher grinds the material in place so there's no burning or hauling. See our land clearing page for more.

My gravel driveway keeps washing out — can you fix it?

Yes. Long lanes on Ozark ground wash when they don't have the right base, crown and drainage to move water off the surface. We regrade and rebuild driveways, add gravel and base, and cut ditches or set culverts so the next hard rain doesn't undo it. See driveway grading and drainage.

Can you prep a building site for a cabin, shop or home?

We do full site work — clearing, cutting the pad, hauling and compacting fill, and shaping the ground so your build sits level and drains right. Then we can pour the concrete slab or footings in-house with our own crew.

What affects the cost of a land-clearing or site-work job near Salem?

Cost depends on the property, not a flat rate. The size of the area, how thick the timber or brush is, the amount of rock in the ground, the slope, how far back and how easy the site is to reach, and the materials involved all factor in. We look at all of that during the free on-site estimate and give you an honest picture before any work starts.

Do you run waterlines or utilities to property back off the road?

Yes. We trench and install waterlines and utilities out to cabins, shops and new builds, and we can bore under driveways, roads and landscaping with directional drilling so we don't tear up what's already there.
